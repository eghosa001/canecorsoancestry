Refunds and billing complaints

You may request cancellation and a refund before review work begins. Once review work has begun, the package fee is not refundable, except where applicable law requires otherwise. Confirmed duplicate or unauthorized charges are eligible for a refund or reversal. If a record is not approved, the fee is not automatically refunded because it covers review, not guaranteed publication or verification.

To request a refund or raise a billing complaint, email support with your Paystack transaction reference, the amount and date, and a short description of the issue. Paystack processing and reversals remain subject to its procedures and applicable law.
