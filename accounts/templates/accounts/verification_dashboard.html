Role referenceWhat Members, Moderators, Senior Moderators and Super Admins can do.

Account and role boundaries

Member identities and staff identities are separate accounts. A kennel/member account can never be promoted into moderation.

Member

Own/manage kennel records, dogs, litters, payments, evidence and disputes. Cannot review or administer other members.

Moderator

Dedicated staff account. Reviews normal submissions, requests evidence, handles disputes and records verification events. No member tools.

Senior Moderator

Moderator powers plus flagged/high-risk decisions, overrides and second approvals. Dog merges are reserved for Super Admin. Cannot manage staff or system rules.

Super Admin

Separate superuser identity. Manages staff accounts, verification rules, protected-record locks and Django administration.
